How to Export Google Timeline

Timeline Visualizer reads your exported Timeline.json file. Google stores Timeline data on your phone — export it first, then upload it here to create your video.

iPhone

Export from Google Maps via Settings → Personal content → Export Timeline data. Works best in Safari.

Open iPhone guide →

Android

Export from Google Maps or Android system settings. Two methods depending on your device.

Open Android guide →

What happens next?

  1. Save Timeline.json on your device.
  2. Open Timeline Visualizer in your browser.
  3. Upload the file, customize your video, and download an MP4.

Your location data stays on your device — nothing is uploaded to our servers.
