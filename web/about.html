About Timeline Visualizer

A free, privacy-first web tool hosted at www.timelinevisualizer.app.

What we do

Timeline Visualizer helps you turn your exported Google Maps location history into an animated travel recap video — entirely in your browser, with no signup and no upload.

Not affiliated with Google

This is an unofficial third-party tool. Google, Google Maps, and Timeline are trademarks of Google LLC. We are not endorsed by or affiliated with Google.

Open source

The core engine is based on google-timeline-visualizer by @mahler83, licensed under the MIT License. See ATTRIBUTION for details.

Why this exists

Google moved Timeline data to on-device storage and removed the web viewer. Millions of users still want to visualize and share their travel history — Timeline Visualizer fills that gap with a simple, private web tool.
